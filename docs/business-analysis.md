# Business analysis

Three documents for one project: a bilingual patient-facing web chatbot for a regional autism services nonprofit. The client, vendor, people, web addresses and system identifiers are replaced with generic descriptions. The two requirements documents open as PDFs, and the user stories are a page on this site.

<div class="sample-cards">
<div class="sample-card">
<div class="cta-group">

[Business requirements document (PDF)](pathname:///business-analysis/Patient-Facing-Chatbot-BRD-Anonymized-Sample.pdf)

</div>

The business case for an English and Spanish website chatbot that answers families' questions 24/7 and turns inquiries into Salesforce leads, with six objectives, their metrics, the stakeholders and the scope. It sets 20 prioritized business requirements, then covers risks, a six-phase delivery plan and open questions.

**Pages:** 7. **Shows:** objectives tied to metrics, requirements tied to objectives, and launch priorities.

</div>
<div class="sample-card">
<div class="cta-group">

[Functional requirements document (PDF)](pathname:///business-analysis/Patient-Facing-Chatbot-FRD-Anonymized-Sample.pdf)

</div>

How the same chatbot behaves, from page-specific greetings and AI answers to the step-by-step inquiry workflow, Salesforce lead capture and Spanish parity. Each functional requirement traces to a business requirement, and a closing table maps all 20 to functional and non-functional requirements.

**Pages:** 12. **Shows:** a scripted conversation flow, field-level Salesforce rules, and full traceability.

<div class="cta-group">

[User stories](./business-analysis-user-stories.md)

</div>

Two epics and 12 user stories that turn the BRD and FRD into work a delivery team can build and test, from starting an inquiry to Spanish parity and crisis routing. Each story has Given, When, Then acceptance criteria and a launch priority, and traces to the requirement IDs in both documents.

**Stories:** 12. **Shows:** acceptance criteria a tester can run, priorities tied to launch, and traceability back to requirements.
