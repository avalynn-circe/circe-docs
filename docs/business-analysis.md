# Business analysis

Two requirements documents for one project: a bilingual patient-facing web chatbot for a regional autism services nonprofit. The client, vendor, people, web addresses and system identifiers are replaced with generic descriptions. Each document opens as a PDF.

<div class="sample-card">
<div class="cta-group">

[Business requirements document (PDF)](pathname:///business-analysis/Patient-Facing-Chatbot-BRD-Anonymized-Sample.pdf)

The business case for an English and Spanish website chatbot that answers families' questions 24/7 and turns inquiries into Salesforce leads, with six objectives, their metrics, the stakeholders and the scope. It sets 20 prioritized business requirements, then covers risks, a six-phase delivery plan and open questions.

**Pages:** 7. **Shows:** objectives tied to metrics, requirements tied to objectives, and launch priorities.

How the same chatbot behaves, from page-specific greetings and AI answers to the step-by-step inquiry workflow, Salesforce lead capture and Spanish parity. Each functional requirement traces to a business requirement, and a closing table maps all 20 to functional and non-functional requirements.

**Pages:** 12. **Shows:** a scripted conversation flow, field-level Salesforce rules, and full traceability.
