# Fleet Lead Generation Report: Nov 2–8, 2026

Nov 10, 2026 · Avalynn Circe

## At a glance

**In one sentence:** our assistants captured 967 leads this week, slightly fewer than last week’s 1,003; the dip came from one technical issue that is now fixed, and senior living had its best week in two months.

**A small dip from one fixed issue; after-hours follow-up is the opportunity**

**What this means**

- **The dip is temporary.** It came from one group of clients during a three-day technical problem, not from fewer interested visitors.
- **Lead quality is steady.** About three in four leads are the kind of person each client wants to hear from.
- **The biggest opportunity is speed after hours.** Four in ten leads arrive at night or on weekends, and they wait almost 14 hours for a call back. The faster someone calls, the more likely they are to book.

## From conversation to lead

The steepest drop is from engaged conversation to starting a lead flow (14.1%); once a flow starts, 57% finish it, so the opportunity is in getting more visitors into a flow, not in the forms themselves.

**One in nineteen conversations becomes a lead**

| Stage | Count | Share of the stage above |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conversations | 18,420 | n/a |
| Engaged (2+ messages) | 11,980 | 65.0% |
| Lead flow started | 1,690 | 14.1% |
| **Lead captured** | **967** | **57.2%** |

Of the 967 captured leads, 929 (96.1%) reached the client CRM; the 38 still queued are all MM-04.

## Lead quality and delivery

Lead quality held steady (72.8% qualified), but delivery slipped: 38 leads are undelivered and after-hours leads wait almost six times longer for a first call.

| Issue | Count | Share of leads | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Duplicate leads (same phone or email within 7 days) | 21 | 2.2% | Merged automatically before sync |
| Invalid or incomplete phone number | 34 | 3.5% | Synced with an “email only” flag |
| Leads lost to the BH-03 incident (estimated) | 30–40 | n/a | 22 recovered through admissions follow-up |
| Wrong-payer conversations needing correction | 58 | n/a | Client notified Nov 7; follow-up list sent |

**Speed to first contact** (from capture to the client’s first logged call or text):

| --- | --- | --- |
| During business hours | 2.4 h | 6.1 h |

With 39% of leads arriving after hours, the overnight wait is the largest quality risk in the fleet that is not a bug.

## Glossary

| Term | Plain meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Lead | A visitor who left their name and a way to reach them |
| Qualified lead | A lead the client confirms is the kind of person they serve |
| Lead rate | Out of 100 conversations, how many became a lead |
| Path | The type of conversation, such as insurance, booking a tour or requesting a call |
| Vertical | The client’s industry: behavioral health (BH), senior living (SL) or multi-site medical (MM) |
| After hours | Evenings, nights and weekends when the client’s staff are not online |
| CRM | The client’s own system for tracking leads and patients |
| Speed to first contact | Time from a lead arriving to the client’s first call or text |
