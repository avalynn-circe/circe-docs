# Interactive lessons

Six interactive lessons. The first four are about Elinor Ostrom's work on governing shared resources. The last two are about your own values. Each lesson is a single HTML page with no server code. It saves your progress in your own browser.

<div class="sample-cards">
<div class="sample-card">
<div class="cta-group">

[Saving Lake Wren](pathname:///lessons/lake-wren.html)

</div>

Five households share one lake. Discover the eight design principles of commons that last.

**Teaches:** Ostrom's eight design principles for commons that last. **You:** run one fishing boat, then rebuild the lake one rule at a time over eight seasons.

</div>
<div class="sample-card">
<div class="cta-group">

[Larkspur in Layers](pathname:///lessons/larkspur-in-layers.html)

</div>

Redraw police, lab, and flood districts to see why polycentric governance works.

**Teaches:** polycentric governance, the Ostroms' case for big and small governments at the same time. **You:** pick the size of each city service for 64 blocks and run a year.

</div>
<div class="sample-card">
<div class="cta-group">

[The Kettle Street Fund](pathname:///lessons/kettle-street-fund.html)

</div>

Twelve houses, one shared fund. How trust, reputation, and norms shape cooperation.

**Teaches:** Ostrom's behavioral theory of the individual: people who learn, follow norms, and differ from each other. **You:** choose what to put into a shared street fund each month, then pull the levers that keep it alive.

</div>
<div class="sample-card">
<div class="cta-group">

[The Juniper Falls Charter](pathname:///lessons/juniper-falls-charter.html)

</div>

Match each resource to the right way of governing it: market, club, state, community, or layered.

**Teaches:** types of goods and five ways of governing them. **You:** stamp each shared thing in a new town's charter and run ten years to see which stamps hold.

</div>
<div class="sample-card">
<div class="cta-group">

[Crosscurrents](pathname:///lessons/crosscurrents.html)

</div>

Map your own values and find the pairs that pull against each other.

**Teaches:** why holding two good values can hurt, and how multidimensional scaling draws a map of where they collide. **You:** face your own values two at a time and read the map.

</div>
<div class="sample-card">
<div class="cta-group">

[Desire Lines](pathname:///lessons/desire-lines.html)

</div>

Name your ideal, make a run of choices, and see how close you got.

**Teaches:** the gap between the values you state and the choices you make. **You:** name an ideal, make 25 ordinary choices, and compare the path you named with the trail you wore.

</div>
</div>
