# Build a deposition outline from tagged exhibits

The outline builder turns documents tagged for one witness into a topic outline with exhibit numbers assigned. After this page you can build an outline and export it as a Word document.

## Before you start

You need the **Reviewer** role or higher.

Tag each document you plan to use with the witness name under **Witness**. Tag the topic under **Deposition topic** if you want the outline grouped by topic.

## Steps

1. Open the matter and go to **Depositions**.
2. Select **New outline** and choose the witness.
3. Quillon lists every document tagged for that witness, grouped by deposition topic. Drag documents to reorder them inside a topic.
4. Select **Number exhibits**. Enter the starting exhibit number. Quillon numbers in outline order.
5. Add questions under each exhibit in the **Notes** field.
6. Select **Export** and choose **Word (.docx)**. The export includes each exhibit's number, Bates range, and notes.

## Limits

- Exhibit numbers are fixed once you export. Renumbering after export creates a new version of the outline and keeps the old one.
- The outline includes produced documents only. Withheld documents do not appear.
