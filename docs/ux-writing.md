# UX writing sample

Six screens for Ovenbird, a fictional asset manager, rewritten for three audiences: individual investors, financial advisors, and institutional clients. The sample is a single HTML page with no server code.

<div class="cta-group">

</div>

## What's in it

- Six screens: onboarding, a market drop alert, a failed transfer, an advisor empty state, a required minimum distribution (RMD) explainer, and a fee change email. Each screen switches between the rewrite, a redline, and the original, with numbered notes that explain every change.
- Tone that changes with the moment. The market alert reads differently for a 3%, 8%, or 20% drop, and the fee email is written three ways for three audiences.
- Accessibility notes and metadata on demand, with live character counts for push notifications, email subject lines, title tags, and search previews.
- A reading grade for each screen, calculated in your browser from the sentences on screen.
- A voice and tone guide for the brand, and a content critique that shows how Avalynn gives feedback.

You can link straight to a screen. For example, [the market drop alert](pathname:///ux-writing-sample/index.html#market-drop) or [the fee change email](pathname:///ux-writing-sample/index.html#fee-notice).

Ovenbird, its funds, balances, and phone numbers are invented. Nothing in the sample is investment, tax, or legal advice.
