Important safety information

Read and understand every safety message before you set up or operate the Northfen PW-1900E Electric Pressure Washer.

Signal words

Safety messages in this manual use these signal words:

DANGER
A hazardous situation that, if not avoided, will cause death or serious injury.
WARNING
A hazardous situation that, if not avoided, could cause death or serious injury.
NOTICE
A situation that could damage the machine or other property but will not cause injury.

Safety messages for this machine

Warning
Injection hazard from high-pressure spray.
The spray can cut skin and inject water and chemicals into the body. An injection can look like a small cut but can cause serious injury or loss of a limb.
Never point the spray gun at yourself, other people or animals.
Never put your hand or fingers in front of the nozzle.
Engage the trigger lock whenever you are not spraying.
If spray pierces the skin, get emergency medical care right away. Tell the doctor it is an injection injury.
Warning
Electric shock.
Water and electricity together can cause death or serious injury.
Plug the power cord only into a grounded outlet.
Keep the plug and the GFCI device on the cord out of water.
Test the GFCI device before each use. Do not use the machine if the test fails.
Do not use an extension cord.
Warning
Stored pressure.
The hose and spray gun stay pressurized after you stop the machine. Disconnecting a pressurized part can release a burst of water and cause injury.
Relieve pressure before you disconnect the hose, change a nozzle, or service the machine.

Personal protective equipment

Wear this equipment every time you operate the machine:

  • Safety glasses or goggles that meet ANSI Z87.1
  • Closed-toe, slip-resistant shoes
  • Long pants
