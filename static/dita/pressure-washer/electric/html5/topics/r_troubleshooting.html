Troubleshooting

Find the symptom in the table, then try each solution in order. If the problem continues, contact an authorized service center.

Warning
Stored pressure.
The hose and spray gun stay pressurized after you stop the machine. Disconnecting a pressurized part can release a burst of water and cause injury.
Relieve pressure before you disconnect the hose, change a nozzle, or service the machine.

Before you inspect or service any part, complete Shut down, relieve pressure and store the pressure washer through the step that relieves pressure.

Table 1. Common problems and solutions
Problem Possible cause Solution
Motor does not start GFCI device has tripped Press RESET on the GFCI device. If it trips again, stop using the machine and contact service.
Circuit breaker has tripped Reset the breaker. Make sure no other devices run on the same circuit.
Low or pulsing pressure Not enough water supply Open the water supply fully. Use a hose no longer than 50 ft (15 m) and check it for kinks.
Air in the system Stop the machine and purge air as described in Set up and start the pressure washer.
Nozzle is worn or partly blocked Clear the nozzle with the cleaning pin, or replace it.
Water leaks at a hose connection Connection is loose or O-ring is damaged Tighten the connection by hand. Replace the O-ring if the leak continues.
Water drips from under the pump Thermal relief valve is releasing hot water This is normal after a long pause in bypass mode. Squeeze the trigger more often, or stop the machine during long breaks.
Detergent does not flow High-pressure nozzle is installed Install the black 65° soap nozzle. Detergent flows only at low pressure.
