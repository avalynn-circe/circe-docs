Set up and start the pressure washer

Connect the water supply and hoses, purge air from the system, and start the PW-1900E.

You need:

  • A garden hose no longer than 50 ft (15 m) and a water supply of at least 1.5 gal/min (5.7 L/min)
  • A grounded 120 V outlet on a 15 A circuit
  • The personal protective equipment listed in Important safety information
Warning
Electric shock.
Water and electricity together can cause death or serious injury.
Plug the power cord only into a grounded outlet.
Keep the plug and the GFCI device on the cord out of water.
Test the GFCI device before each use. Do not use the machine if the test fails.
Do not use an extension cord.

Place the machine on a level surface outdoors, where spray and runoff cannot reach electrical outlets or open drains.

  1. Check that the inlet screen is clean and seated in the garden hose inlet.

    Rinse the screen under running water if you see debris.

  2. Connect the garden hose to the garden hose inlet and tighten it by hand.
  3. Connect the high-pressure hose to the pump outlet and to the spray gun.

    Pull back on each quick-connect fitting to confirm it is locked.

  4. Insert a nozzle into the end of the spray wand.

    For a first test, use the white 40° nozzle. See Choosing a nozzle.

  5. Notice
    Pump damage.
    Running the pump without water overheats the seals in seconds.
    Turn on the water supply and purge air from the system before you start the machine.
    Turn on the water supply fully. Release the trigger lock, point the spray gun at the ground, and squeeze the trigger until a steady stream of water flows with no air sputtering.

    Air is purged from the pump and hoses.

  6. Plug the power cord into the outlet and press TEST and then RESET on the GFCI device.

    The GFCI indicator light turns on.

  7. Turn the power switch to ON.
  8. Warning
    Injection hazard from high-pressure spray.
    The spray can cut skin and inject water and chemicals into the body. An injection can look like a small cut but can cause serious injury or loss of a limb.
    Never point the spray gun at yourself, other people or animals.
    Never put your hand or fingers in front of the nozzle.
    Engage the trigger lock whenever you are not spraying.
    If spray pierces the skin, get emergency medical care right away. Tell the doctor it is an injection injury.
    Hold the spray gun with both hands and squeeze the trigger to begin cleaning.

    Start with the nozzle at least 24 in. (60 cm) from the surface and move closer slowly. Engage the trigger lock whenever you pause.

The pressure washer is running and ready for use. When you finish, follow Shut down, relieve pressure and store the pressure washer.
