Shut down, relieve pressure and store the pressure washer

Stop the PW-1900E, release trapped pressure, and drain the pump so the machine is safe to handle and ready for storage.

Follow these steps every time you finish using the machine, and before you change a nozzle, clean the inlet screen, or perform any maintenance.

Warning
Stored pressure.
The hose and spray gun stay pressurized after you stop the machine. Disconnecting a pressurized part can release a burst of water and cause injury.
Relieve pressure before you disconnect the hose, change a nozzle, or service the machine.
  1. Engage the trigger lock on the spray gun.
  2. Turn the power switch to OFF and unplug the power cord.
  3. Turn off the water supply.
  4. Warning
    Injection hazard from high-pressure spray.
    The spray can cut skin and inject water and chemicals into the body. An injection can look like a small cut but can cause serious injury or loss of a limb.
    Never point the spray gun at yourself, other people or animals.
    Never put your hand or fingers in front of the nozzle.
    Engage the trigger lock whenever you are not spraying.
    If spray pierces the skin, get emergency medical care right away. Tell the doctor it is an injection injury.
    Point the spray gun at the ground, release the trigger lock, and squeeze the trigger until water stops flowing.

    Pressure is relieved from the hose and spray gun.

  5. Engage the trigger lock again.
  6. Disconnect the garden hose, the high-pressure hose, and the spray gun.
  7. Drain the water from the pump, hoses and spray gun.

    Tip the machine so the pump outlet faces down, and hold each hose vertically until it stops dripping.

  8. Notice
    Freeze damage.
    Water left in the pump can freeze, crack the pump and void the warranty.
    Drain all water and add pump protector before you store the machine where temperatures can fall below 32 °F (0 °C).
    If you will store the machine for more than 30 days or below freezing, add pump protector.

    Attach the pump protector bottle to the garden hose inlet and squeeze until foam comes out of the pump outlet.

  9. Store the machine and accessories indoors in a dry place.

The machine is depressurized, drained and protected for storage.
