Round zero · The Saturday problem

Thursday night, two good things collide

Weeks ago you promised your oldest friend you'd help them move on Saturday. Tonight a client offers paid work worth a month of rent, but only if you start Saturday morning. What do you do?

Pick what you'd really do. There's no right answer.

How true is this of you? "I keep my promises to the people I love."

And this? "I make sure I'm financially secure."

What happens to my answers?

Everything you choose or write stays in this browser tab. Nothing is sent anywhere, and closing the tab erases it. This is a reflection exercise, not a test. It doesn't score you, label you, or say anything about your health.
